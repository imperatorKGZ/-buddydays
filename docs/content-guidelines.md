# BuddyDays content guidelines

- Write for dog and cat owners in the United States using clear US English.
- Start each article with the reader's practical question or problem.
- Keep categories focused; use tags for cross-cutting subjects.
- Do not invent veterinary expertise, product testing, personal experiences, or sources.
- Health, nutrition, medication, and behavior topics require appropriate authoritative sources and extra review.
- Draft entries remain `status: "draft"` until the complete article has been reviewed and approved.
- Only `status: "published"` articles are included in production listings and indexing.
- Every published article needs an accurate title, summary, category, tags, reading time, and reviewed body content.
