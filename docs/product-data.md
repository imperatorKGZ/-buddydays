# Product Data

## Product
BuddyDays-owned catalog entity. Contains stable specifications and traceable source references. No Amazon price, inventory, Amazon image, or merchant affiliate URL.

## Offer
Merchant-specific purchase route associated with a Product. Store only data allowed by that merchant's program. A stored link does not guarantee current stock or price.

## ProductSource
Evidence record with source type, title, URL/document path, checked date, supported fields, and notes.

## Images
Use original, commissioned, licensed, or explicitly permitted images. Record rights basis. Do not copy Amazon Product Advertising Content into `public/`.

## Verification
Use `active`, `needs-review`, and `discontinued` statuses. Important claims need sources and a meaningful verification date.
