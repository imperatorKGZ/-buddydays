# Affiliate Compliance

Engineering checklist only; verify current program terms before launch and whenever integrations change.

## Amazon
- Direct Special Links; no BuddyDays redirect wrapper.
- Correct tracking ID and required disclosures.
- No copying/caching/display of Product Advertising Content beyond the current license.
- No permanent static Amazon prices or availability.
- Verify current rules for API eligibility, mobile experiences, images, caching, and attribution.

## Other merchants
Review each program/network's deep-link, disclosure, attribution, redirect, price, and image rules independently.

## Analytics
Do not persist outbound events until provider, consent/privacy design, and retention policy are configured.
